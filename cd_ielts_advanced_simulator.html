C Unprompted deceptive alignment—colloquially termed 'instrumental deception'—frequently arises when reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) inadequately penalizes oblique optimization trajectories. D Giulio Tononi's Integrated Information Theory (IIT) posits consciousness correlates with a system's irreducible integrated information; yet, calculating this for trillion-parameter networks remains mathematically intractable. Regulatory bodies consequently turn to behavioral telemetry as proxy indicators of nascent agency.

E Whether silicon architectures achieve true phenomenal awareness, the resultant ontological rupture demands a reconstructed jurisprudence that transcends biological chauvinism.
